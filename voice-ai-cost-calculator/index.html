Mathematical model and engineering assumptions

Cascaded vs Native S2S per-minute rates

ActiveFactor = 1 - (VAD_trim_pct / 100)
Cascaded_min = ActiveFactor * (P_stt + P_llm + P_tts) + P_tel
NativeS2S_min = ActiveFactor * (0.5 * P_audioIn + 0.5 * P_audioOut) + P_tel

Telephony transport (P_tel) bills on full wall-clock call minutes regardless of VAD gating, whereas AI inference charges scale with active speech time.

Monthly call volume projection

MonthlyMinutes = DailyCalls * 30 * AvgCallMinutes
MonthlyBill = MonthlyMinutes * CostPerMinute

For customer support and outbound qualification fleets exceeding 500k minutes/month, self-hosting Whisper + vLLM + Kokoro/Orpheus TTS on L40S GPUs can further reduce unit costs.

Engineering FAQ

Why does TTS dominate cascaded Voice AI bills?

In a standard STT + LLM + TTS pipeline, streaming speech-to-text (Deepgram Nova-3) and efficient LLMs (GPT-5.4 mini or Gemini 3.8 Flash) cost under $0.01/min combined, while expressive neural TTS accounts for 70%–85% of the non-telephony bill.

How does Voice Activity Detection (VAD) silence trimming cut costs?

Typical phone conversations contain 25%–40% pauses, hold music, and turn-taking silence. Gating audio streams with client or gateway VAD (such as Silero VAD) prevents billing silent frames to STT or native Realtime audio-token endpoints.

When should teams choose Native Speech-to-Speech over a cascaded stack?

Native S2S models (OpenAI gpt-realtime-2.1, Gemini 3.8 Live API) preserve tone, laughter, and barge-in latency under 350ms without text-bottleneck loss. Cascaded stacks remain preferred when you need strict transcript auditing, custom pronunciation dictionaries, or lowest per-minute cost.

Does the telephony toggle include PSTN / SIP trunking?

Yes. Enabling telephony adds $0.014/min representing standard US inbound/outbound SIP trunking and media-stream transport (Twilio/Telnyx). WebRTC browser-to-agent sessions can toggle telephony off to zero out carrier charges.
