Engineering FAQ

Why does Cloudflare R2 stay flat on the egress cliff chart?

Cloudflare R2 charges $0.00 per GB of egress bandwidth regardless of monthly outbound volume. As a result, its monthly bill is determined solely by stored terabytes and Class A/B API operations, eliminating hyperscaler egress spikes.

How does Backblaze B2 3x free egress work?

Backblaze B2 includes free monthly egress up to 3x your average stored capacity (for example, 40 TB stored includes 120 TB of free monthly download). Egress beyond that threshold is billed at $0.01/GB—still roughly 8x to 12x cheaper than AWS S3 or GCS.

Does putting CloudFront in front of AWS S3 eliminate egress fees?

No. While S3-to-CloudFront origin transfer is free, CloudFront still bills internet data transfer out at roughly $0.085/GB in US/EU regions (after the 1 TB free tier). For media, model weights, or dataset distribution, egress dominates 80%+ of the bill.

When do Class A and Class B operation fees overtake storage costs?

Workloads with millions of tiny objects (such as tile maps, small JSON logs, or high-churn feature stores) generate heavy Class A PUT/LIST charges ($4.50–$5.00 per million requests). Batching small objects into Parquet or tar archives cuts operation bills dramatically.
